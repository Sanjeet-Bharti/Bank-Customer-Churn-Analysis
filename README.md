# 🏦 Bank Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn in a bank using Python, SQL, and Power BI. The objective is to identify the key factors that influence customer attrition and provide business insights that can help the bank improve customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze customer churn patterns.
- Identify factors affecting customer retention.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Generate actionable business insights.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- Customers from **Germany** have the highest churn rate.
- Older customers are more likely to leave the bank.
- Active members are less likely to churn.
- Customers with multiple products show different churn patterns.
- Balance and salary alone do not strongly predict churn.
